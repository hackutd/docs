## Welcome!

Whether you're interested in using Jury at your own hackathon or wanting to help develop and improve the software, we're glad you're here 💙. Continue reading for information to get Jury up and running in no time for your event. If you instead want to help develop Jury, go to the [Contributing information](/jury/contributing).
